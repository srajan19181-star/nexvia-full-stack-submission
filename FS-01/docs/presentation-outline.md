6. **5:15–6:15 — Redis scaling:** invalidation fanout, awareness, ordered database replay, missed-message reconciliation.
7. **6:15–7:15 — Comments and history:** relative anchors, replies, resolution, immutable snapshots, safe restore.
8. **7:15–8:15 — Permissions and audit:** role matrix, REST/WebSocket enforcement, activity records.
9. **8:15–9:15 — Testing and DevOps:** integration tests, Playwright, load runner, Docker/health checks.
10. **9:15–10:00 — Results and next steps:** measured report, operational considerations, roadmap.
